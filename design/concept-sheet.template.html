Pro Racer Z

Night falls on the rooftop league. Fly a hover racer across lit roofs and sky bridges against three rival pilots. Finish in the top three or you're out, and spell OVERDRIVE to earn a faster machine.

Your machine

You start in a Mk I. Spell OVERDRIVE to step up. Cars are drawn from above and turned in code, so the art has to be top-down. Set which way each sprite's nose points; the card turns it nose-up when that's right.

Rival pilots

Three every race, each with one habit you can learn and use. Their pace rises a little each race and jumps with each league.

On the track

Pickups float a pixel above the asphalt and come back a few seconds after you take them. Only you collect them. Icons show at half size (8 px) in the top bar, still pixel-exact because the game renders at 4×.

Hazards

Every hazard warns you first. Coolant spills, slicks, boost pads and laser gates are drawn in code; these three are sprites. A shield soaks up one hit from any of them.

Rooftops & the podium

Scenery on the roofs below the track, drawn with a little parallax so the track feels raised. The trophy appears on the results board.

How a season plays

Top three or outFour pilots a race. Finish 1st to 3rd and you go on to the next track. Finish 4th and you spend a retry: the race runs again and its points are lost. You start with two retries. With none left, the season is over.
Darts & minesBoth cost one charge. A dart flies ahead and bends a little towards the nearest pilot in front. A mine drops behind you and arms after half a second; it spins anyone who touches it, you included. Charge cells give two (you can hold nine), and charges carry over between races.
O·V·E·R·D·R·I·V·ETwo or three letters float on each track, and the ones you take stay with you. Spell the word for 25,000 points and the next machine. After the Mk III, each spelling pays 50,000 and a retry.
Thruster · Core · GripUpgrade pickups for acceleration, top speed and cornering, up to level 3 each. They last until you change machine. The pit board between races shows where you stand.
ShieldSoaks up the next spin or crash, from a dart, mine, slick, drone or gate. You can see it round your car.
Dynamo's grudgeEach time you hit a rival, Dynamo's heat goes up one. At five he overdrives for the rest of the race, faster than anything you can build. Heat drops by one each race, so pick your shots.
Warned firstGate posts flash a beat before the beam rises. Drones sweep a red line ahead of them. A storm's shadow reaches the track before its rain does.
Hover handlingNo wheels: the car can turn on the spot, so you never get stuck facing a wall. It slides wide if you carry too much speed into a bend, less with Grip.

From the 1988 original

Pro Racer Z borrows the shape of Rare's R.C. Pro-Am: a top-down race against three rivals, weapons and pickups on the track, letters that earn a faster car, and elimination for last place. The setting, names, cars, tracks, art and sound are new.

Kept
  • Four racers, top three go on, last place is out
  • Weapons ahead and behind, ammo that carries over
  • Letters spelling a word earn a faster vehicle
  • Speed, acceleration and handling pickups
  • Slicks, puddles, rain and pop-up barriers
  • A rival who gets faster if you rely on weapons
  • Two retries that cost the race's points
Changed
  • Night rooftop league; hover racers, not toy trucks
  • A top-down camera that follows and looks ahead, not isometric
  • Three named rivals, each with a habit
  • Three leagues of eight tracks with a real ending
  • Barriers are laser gates on a timer
Added
  • Minimap with every pilot on it
  • Steering by pointing, for sticks and touch
  • Every hazard warns before it bites
  • Shield bubble, boost pads, security drones
  • Lap records, a pit board and a high-score table
  • Controller and touch from day one
